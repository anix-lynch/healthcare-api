# Step-by-Step: Assign Fabric Capacity to Workspace

## You're Here: ✅ Settings Panel Open

I can see you've opened the Settings panel on the right side. Now follow these exact steps:

---

## Step 1: Close This Settings Panel
**This is the wrong settings menu** - this is your personal user settings.

Click anywhere outside the Settings panel to close it, or click the X.

---

## Step 2: Find Workspace Settings (The Gear Icon)

Look at the **top-right corner** of the workspace page, near your profile picture.

You should see:
- **⚙️ Gear icon** (Workspace settings) ← **Click this one!**
- Your profile picture
- Other icons

**Click the ⚙️ Gear icon** (NOT the Settings button you already clicked)

---

## Step 3: In the Workspace Settings Menu

After clicking the gear icon, you'll see a dropdown menu with options like:
- Workspace settings
- Workspace access
- Manage connections and gateways
- etc.

**Click "Workspace settings"**

---

## Step 4: Find the License/Premium Section

In the Workspace settings page, look for one of these tabs/sections on the left:
- **"License mode"** or
- **"Premium"** or
- **"Capacity"** or
- **"Trial"**

The exact name varies, but it's usually near the top of the settings sections.

---

## Step 5: Assign Capacity

Once you find the License/Premium/Capacity section:

1. You should see a dropdown or option that says:
   - "License mode" or "Capacity assignment"
   
2. Select one of:
   - **"Trial"** (if you see this option)
   - **"Fabric capacity"** (then select your trial capacity from dropdown)
   - **"Premium capacity"** (then select your trial capacity)

3. Click **"Save"** or **"Apply"**

---

## Alternative: If You Don't See Premium/License Tab

If you don't see a Premium or License tab in workspace settings, try this:

### Option A: Via Admin Portal
1. Go to: https://app.fabric.microsoft.com/admin-portal/capacities
2. Look for your **Trial capacity** (should show as F64 or similar)
3. Click on the capacity name
4. Under "Workspaces" section, click **"Assign workspaces"**
5. Search for `HealthcareAnalytics`
6. Check the box next to it
7. Click **"Save"**

### Option B: Check Trial Status
1. Go to: https://app.fabric.microsoft.com/admin-portal/trials
2. Verify your trial is active
3. Note the capacity name
4. Go back to workspace and try assigning that capacity

---

## What You're Looking For

The setting should look something like:

```
License mode: [Dropdown]
  ○ Pro
  ○ Premium Per User  
  ● Trial ← Select this
  ○ Fabric capacity
```

Or:

```
Capacity: [Dropdown]
  Select a capacity...
  ● Trial-F64 ← Select your trial capacity
```

---

## After Assigning Capacity

Once saved, you should be able to:
1. Click **"+ New"** in the workspace
2. See **"Warehouse"** option under Data Engineering
3. Create the warehouse
